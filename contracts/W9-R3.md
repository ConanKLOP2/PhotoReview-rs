## Semantics (C# as implemented is the spec)

Verification, reconciliation, recovery retry and Undo must judge source and destination by stored spelling for a case-only pair. A third spelling stays ambiguous, never Committed or Lost. Group undo restores each mapped member; normal Move/Copy behavior remains intact.
