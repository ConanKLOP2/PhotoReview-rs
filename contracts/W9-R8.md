## Semantics (C# as implemented is the spec)

Modal dialog preselects stem, shows immutable extension to right, validates on EN_CHANGE and again on OK, disables Rename on error, Enter accepts only valid, Escape cancels. Error line grows/shrinks without overlap. Both F2 and menu dispatch same command. Settings mouse/shortcut pages expose the new entries.
