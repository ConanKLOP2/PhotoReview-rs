    48	    /// Navigation and zoom keys are deliberately not listed so they keep repeating for fast browsing.
    49	    /// ToggleInfoOverlay is listed too: it flips and SAVES a setting, so holding the key would flicker the overlay
    50	    /// and rewrite config.json on every repeat. Fullscreen, ToggleCompare and ClickZoom are pure toggles: holding the key
    51	    /// would flip them at the key-repeat rate (and re-present the image for ToggleCompare), so they act once.
    52	    /// ToggleKeepZoom (PR-B) is the same kind of toggle-and-save as ToggleInfoOverlay. OpenFolder (Q-R42) and
    53	    /// CustomZoom (Q-R43) each open a modal dialog: holding the key must not stack up several of them. Refresh may start a
    54	    /// full-resolution decode: once per press is enough.
