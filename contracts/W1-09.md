- Semantics notes:
  1. **No behaviour except `matches_moved_destination`.** Every `new` just stores its arguments and the documented defaults; do not add validation (C# records
     accept any value, e.g. empty strings and negative sizes).
  2. **C# defaults** (the ones that are NOT `false`/`null`): `FileActionResult.journal_persisted = true`, `CaptureGroupMemberResult.state_known = true`,
     `CaptureGroupActionResult.journal_persisted = true`. Wrong defaults here silently change recovery behaviour -- test them.
  3. **matches_moved_destination(stat, size, recorded_utc)**: `stat.length == size` as exact equality between `u64` and `i64` (convert with
     `i64::try_from(stat.length)`; a length above `i64::MAX` never matches) AND `|stat.last_write_utc.ticks() - recorded_utc.ticks()| <= 20_000_000`
     (2 s, inclusive; compute with `i64::abs_diff` or `checked_sub`; both values are valid `DateTimeUtc` ticks in `0..=3_155_378_975_999_999_999`, so no overflow, but do not use `as` casts).
     Direction does not matter (earlier and later are equal). FAT/exFAT may round a moved file's write time; a file that never left its volume is compared exactly by the CALLERS, not here.
  4. **Equality** is derived value equality (C# record equality): strings compared exactly (case-sensitive), `Option`s by value. `CaptureGroupActionResult` derives only `PartialEq`
     (not `Eq`) because `JournalEntry` may not be `Eq`; keep that unless the lead says `JournalEntry: Eq`.
  5. **`operation` in `UndoResult` is `Option<FileOperationType>`** (C# `FileOperationType?`): `None` = nothing to undo.
  6. **Paths are `String`** (journaled as text, compared exactly). Do not convert to `PathBuf`.
  7. **`CaptureGroupActionResult.entry`** is `Option<Box<JournalEntry>>` (boxed to keep the type small; derive/clone work through the box).
- Traps checklist: §7 items 3 (no overflow / no `as` casts between `u64` and `i64` sizes), 5 (time: ticks via `DateTimeUtc`, never `SystemTime`), 8 (`None` vs `Some("")` for error/destination text).
