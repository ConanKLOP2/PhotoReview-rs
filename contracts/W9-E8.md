## Traps
- Owner HWND and every dialog call stay on the UI thread; no `async`, nothing is cached across calls.
- `PromptAddExternalEditor` returns an editor whose `id` is the `ExternalEditor::new()` one: the caller (`MainViewModel::add_external_editor`) assigns a fresh id anyway.
- A missing file-dialog service is `None`, not an error and not a panic.
- Do not conflate `SecondaryWindowKind::RenamePrompt` (FEAT-RENAME) with this feature.
