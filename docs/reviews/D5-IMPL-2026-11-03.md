# D5 IMPL: bounded, delayed retry of `apply_update_result` (P-321)

Date: 2026-11-03. Owner decision P-321 (follow the recommendation). Crate: `pr-platform-win`.

## What changed

`crates/pr-platform-win/src/dialogs/settings/pages/general.rs`:

- `apply_update_result` used to re-post itself at once, without limit, while the presenter was borrowed. It now waits
  `UPDATE_RESULT_RETRY_DELAY` (20 ms) between tries and stops after `UPDATE_RESULT_MAX_TRIES` (50 tries in all, the first
  included, so at most 49 waits). At the cap it gives up with one log line (`AppLog` warn) and applies nothing.
- New seam `UpdateRetryScheduler` (`after(delay, job)`, `give_up(tries)`). Production `ThreadRetryScheduler` sleeps on a
  short-lived `settings-update-retry` thread, then posts through the window poster (a closed window drops the post).
  `GeneralSettingsPage::set_retry_scheduler` lets tests inject a fake clock.

## Deviation vs C#

C# `GeneralSettingsPage` has no such loop (its `async` continuation runs on the UI thread and cannot meet a borrowed
presenter). Rust-only hardening, no C# change.

Suggested PARITY row (Different): "Settings General page: a busy presenter makes the update answer retry every 20 ms, 50 tries
at most, then log and drop (Rust-only hardening of the cross-thread post; C# has no loop). ADR D5 / P-321."

Suggested ADR text: "The update check answer is posted to the Settings UI thread. If the presenter is borrowed when it
runs, the answer is retried after 20 ms, up to 50 tries in total, then dropped with a log line. Before, it was re-posted
immediately and without bound (a busy loop). C# has no equivalent. Consequence: after a give-up the status line keeps its
'checking' text and the check button stays disabled until the window is reopened (not widened here; 1 s of continuous
busy presenter is not expected in practice)."

## Tests (`tests/w8_settingswin4_killers.rs`)

- `update_result_that_cannot_apply_retries_with_a_delay_and_gives_up_at_the_cap`: presenter held borrowed, fake clock
  records waits; asserts no immediate re-post, exactly 50 tries, 49 waits of 20 ms, one give-up with 50, no control changed.
  Red check: with the production loop replaced by the old immediate re-post it FAILED; green with the change.
- `update_result_applies_on_a_later_try_once_the_presenter_is_free`: one busy try, then applied after the wait, no give-up.
