## Proposed rows (for docs/PROPOSED-DECISIONS.md, added by the lead)

- P-340: `SharedDialogService` (pr-shell adapters) must forward `prompt_add_external_editor`, `prompt_default_external_editor` and `prompt_rename` to the wrapped `Win32DialogService`, with a test that lists every `DialogService` member (red when a member is added and not forwarded). Production behaviour change; affects "Add more editors...", "Set default editor..." through the view model. Crate `pr-shell`.
- P-341: `LazySecondaryPort::get` wraps the factory in `catch_unwind` and answers `None` like C# `Port()`; decide whether a panicked factory is retried (keep the factory) or kept as `None`. Production change in `pr-platform-win`.
- P-342: `schedule_on_thread` (cloak): when the deadline thread cannot be spawned, reveal the window at once instead of only cancelling. Production change in `pr-platform-win`; needs a spawn seam to be testable.
- P-343: decide whether `StartupTask::join` failures (panic) are logged by the consumers (`phase_one`, `run`) or stay silent as an optimisation fallback; low priority, likely wontfix.
