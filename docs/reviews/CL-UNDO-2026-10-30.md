## The remaining gap (why the row stays Partial)

C# `GetFileStat` throws for an unreadable or over-long path; the seam `FileSystem::try_get_file_stat` (P-12) reproduces that as `Err`.
In `undo.rs` every Move site already goes through `CaseOnlyRename::stat_as`/`exists_as` and the seam. Three sites still call the
helper `UndoService::stat` (`get_file_stat`, which turns `Err` into `None`):

- `undo.rs:810` single Recycle: stat of the source after the restore;
- `undo.rs:1112` group Recycle: stat of the member source before the restore;
- `undo.rs:1177` group Recycle: stat of the member source after the restore.

For such a path Rust reports "not restored" / a missing file where C# raises the stat error. Fix = route `stat` through
`try_get_file_stat` and map `Err` to the undo failure text (production change, test first, with the C# `UndoServiceTests` stat-failure
expectations); not done in this lane.
