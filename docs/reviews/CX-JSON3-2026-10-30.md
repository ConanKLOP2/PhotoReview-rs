## Method

CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0, CARGO_PROFILE_DEV_DEBUG=line-tables-only, build jobs 2.
One cargo-mutants process at a time; jobs 1, baseline skip, 90 seconds per
mutant. A single outer `timeout 1200` bounds the entire four-group sequence.

over the generic mutants skill wrapper defaults. No normal test uses target-mut.
