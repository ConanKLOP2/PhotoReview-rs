## Killers

The new retry tests use fake move and delay callbacks, no disk or clock:

- `replace_with_retry_transient_failure_stops_after_eight_attempts`: access denied,
  sharing and lock violation each perform exactly eight calls, seven delays, and preserve
  the final OS error. Assertions bound calls and sleeps even if a counter stops advancing.
- `replace_with_retry_success_and_nontransient_error_finish_without_extra_calls`:
  two transient failures followed by success; errors 2, 3, 87 and 1314 propagate without
  sleeping. Delay values must be 3 and 6. Comments identify source lines and operators.

The retry mutation command uses the killers binary alone, proving its own detection rather
than relying on earlier integration tests. No wall-clock assertion or sleep is introduced.
