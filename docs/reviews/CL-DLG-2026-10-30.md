The first run (39 mutants, 4 small files) used too few binaries and showed 16 MISSED (`classify_command` arms, `locate_focus`, `focus_field`, `reset_active_page`, `on_closed`, `LoadingGuard` Debug). Re-running with the full binary list above (settings_dialog_layout contains the `on_closed` test, the goldens cover the rest) caught all of them: they were a test-selection artifact, not coverage gaps.
