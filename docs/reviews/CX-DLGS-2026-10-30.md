## Method

Normal builds: `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=<worktree>/target`; mutation:
`CARGO_TARGET_DIR=<worktree>/target-mut`, `--output mut-out-GROUP`.
`CARGO_INCREMENTAL=0`, `CARGO_PROFILE_DEV_DEBUG=line-tables-only`, build jobs 2.
One owned cargo-mutants process at a time, `--baseline skip --timeout 90 -j 1`.
Each group is listed before running; compare its inventory to Found.
Outer timeout bounds each invocation; shell initial + proof are bounded to
1200 + 660 seconds (actual initial 9 minutes; combined target about 20 minutes).
No normal cargo test uses target-mut. New tests use TempRoot/fakes and fixed
clock; no sleeps, fixture mtime assumptions or wall-clock dependence.
